The name is the thesis

Compass points the way.
So — Labs of what?

Every great infra company is a "labs" of one big idea. Stripe: labs of internet payments. OpenAI: labs of intelligence. Us?

Compass Labs is the lab for what finance becomes
when anyone can launch and operate
financial products onchain.

Not "a DeFi API." Not "an RWA company." Those are features of the thing, not the thing. The thing is a direction: a world where a fintech in Mexico City, a wallet in Lagos, a fund in London can stand up real financial products — yield, credit, trading, tokenized assets, agentic execution — as easily as they add a login. We're building the instrument that points every one of them the same way: onchain, non-custodial, live in days.

Why this matters now

Money is moving onchain faster than
anyone can build products for it.

$35 trillion moved on-chain last year. Stablecoins stopped being a crypto category and became infrastructure. Every fintech now wants to hold digital dollars — and almost none of them can do anything with those dollars, because building each product is a year of engineering and an audit.

The world got the rails.
It's still missing the products.

That gap — between the money being onchain and anyone being able to use it — is the biggest opportunity in fintech right now. It's also exactly the gap we sit in. We don't have to invent the demand. It's already here, pounding on the door, in every market at once.

Beyond the tech

We facilitate much more than code.

The trap is to describe ourselves as "the technology." The tech is the easy part. What actually blocks a fintech from launching real financial products is everything around the tech — and that's what we take off their plate.

Access & eligibility

Who is allowed to touch each product, in each country, under which rules — enforced by the infrastructure.

Compliance-aware distribution

Regulated products (gov bonds, tokenized treasuries) reaching the right buyers, safely, reusably.

Whitelisting & controls

Address-level permissions and policy — the boring, critical guardrails institutions require.

Partnerships & venues

Every new venue we integrate is a product our customers get for free. We do the deals; they get the reach.

Everyone else sells you a piece. We make you able to operate. That single sentence is the whole difference — and the whole moat.

The endgame · how big this actually gets

Money is moving onchain. We want to be the layer it all runs through.

Look at the direction of the world: stablecoins and crypto are landing in more and more balances every year — people, businesses, apps. That's not a trend, it's a migration. And when everyone holds value onchain, someone has to be the infrastructure that lets any app do something with it — earn, borrow, trade, invest, move. We want that layer to be Compass.

The long game: connect everyone to onchain finance —
and be the rails underneath, invisible.

That's the size of the mission. But you don't get there by announcing it — you get there one integration at a time. So right now, the job is concrete: win this generation of clients (fintechs, wallets, stablecoin apps), build the partnerships and venues that make us more valuable with every one, and be the trusted infrastructure underneath them. Nail that, and the bigger world connects itself.

The world if we win

Picture it in five years.

A neobank in Buenos Aires launches dollar-yield on a Tuesday — no blockchain team, no audit season, no custody risk. A wallet in Nairobi adds tokenized treasuries the way it once added a QR code. A fund in London distributes a regulated product across a dozen apps without rebuilding compliance for each. None of them mention "DeFi." None of them see the chain.

They just shipped a financial product.
Compass was underneath the whole time.

That's the win: not that people talk about us — that they can't imagine building without us, and mostly forget we're there. The best infrastructure is invisible. That's the direction the needle points.
