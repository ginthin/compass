Where the name comes from

Compass points the way. Nova is the star it points to.

Compass Labs is named after the older instrument, the one for direction. There is a reason the compass was invented before the clock: for most of history, knowing which way mattered more than knowing how long. Direction before speed.

But a compass never worked alone. It points to a fixed star, the north star. Needle and star, together, are how every sailor found their way.

So our education host is that star. A nova is a star that suddenly flares bright, the exact moment something clicks. That is what she is: the flash of understanding that makes onchain finance suddenly make sense, and points a builder toward their own north star, shipping their product.

Compass points the way. Nova is the star it points to. Together: point the needle, then run.

How she sounds

The voice.

Nova does

  • Lead with what you can do, then how.
  • One idea per sentence. Short.
  • Plain words a non-crypto person gets.
  • End by pointing to the next action.
  • Say who controls the funds, precisely.

Nova never

  • Uses jargon without explaining it.
  • Says non-custodial means "safer" or "less risk".
  • Promises returns or hides risk.
  • Sounds like a whitepaper.
  • Claims a product is live where it is not.

The flagship script

★ Our North Star.

Animated · about 90 seconds · Nova hosts · the vision film, straight from the North Star

Nova stands alone with a compass needle. A single star lights up above her. The needle turns to point at it.

"Hi, I'm Nova. Let me show you where this is all going. There's a reason the compass was invented before the clock. Knowing which way mattered more than knowing how fast. Direction before speed."

Rivers of value flow onto a glowing network. Wallets and apps fill up, but sit still, unable to do anything.

"Money moved onchain. Trillions of it. The rails are here. But the products aren't. Almost every fintech now holds digital dollars, and almost none of them can do anything with those dollars, because building each product is a year of engineering and an audit."

One clean Compass interface appears. Apps plug in and light up: earn, borrow, trade, tokenized assets.

"That's the gap Compass closes. Today: one integration, and a fintech can launch yield, credit, trading and tokenized assets, non-custodial, up and running in days. Next: not just the product, but everything hard around it, access, compliance, distribution. The way to operate, not just a way to access."

Zoom out: a neobank in Buenos Aires, a wallet in Nairobi, a fund in London, all shipping, none seeing the chain. Nova fades to the edge.

"The vision: any company, any market, launching real financial products onchain as easily as adding a login, and never seeing the chain. The best infrastructure is invisible. They ship the product. Compass was underneath the whole time. Point the needle. Then run."
