Meet Norte — your
AI marketing co-pilot.

You don't have to be a marketer. When you need a post, an email, or an outreach message, you just ask Norte and it writes it — in the Compass voice, with the right facts. Here's how it works, in 3 minutes.

What is Norte?

It's Compass's marketing brain, in an AI you can talk to.

Norte is an AI assistant that already knows how Compass talks, what we're allowed to claim, and our three core messages. Everyone can use it — Eli, Aidar, Funa, Gin. You open your own Claude, tell Norte what you need, and it drafts it for you. You review and post. That's it.

Why this matters: content no longer bottlenecks on one person. Anyone can generate an on-brand post in 30 seconds. Gin runs the big marketing + the official accounts — but for your own posts and outreach, you're self-serve.
How you use it · dead simple

Ask Norte. It writes. You post.

You talk to Norte in plain language — no commands to memorize. Just tell it what happened or what you need. Examples of exactly what you'd type and what comes back:

YOU TYPE"Norte, write me a LinkedIn post — we just went live with IXS on BNB Chain."
NORTE RETURNSA finished LinkedIn post in the Compass voice, using approved claims, with one clear CTA. Two versions (safe + sharper). You pick one, tweak, post.
YOU TYPE"Norte, a customer asked if we hold the funds. Give me a short post answering it."
NORTE RETURNSAn "Objection of the Week" style post: "Do you hold the funds? No. Users sign in their own wallets…" — ready to post or turn into a 30-sec video.
YOU TYPE"Norte, I'm messaging a fintech in Colombia about yield. Draft me a short outreach note."
NORTE RETURNSA 3-sentence personalized opener you can send from LinkedIn or email.

You never format, you never worry about the wording or the claims — Norte handles all of that. You just say what you need in normal words.

Get set up · 2 minutes, once

Turn your Claude into Norte.

  1. Open Claude (web or app) in your account.
  2. Paste the Norte prompt below as your first message. (Gin will share it — it's also in the Claims Library doc.)
  3. That's it — now just talk to Norte normally: "write me a post about…", "draft an opener for…", etc.

Do this once and keep the chat, or paste the prompt at the top whenever you start a new one. The prompt IS Norte — it carries the voice, the messages, and the safe facts.

▸ Show the Norte prompt (copy this) 
You are Norte, Compass Labs' marketing co-pilot. Write in our voice, using only approved facts.

WHAT COMPASS IS (never improvise a new version):
"Compass lets fintechs, wallets and stablecoin platforms launch yield, credit,
trading and tokenized-asset products through one integration — non-custodial,
live in days." Compass is the LAYER UNDERNEATH; the fintech is the hero.

VOICE: Clear like Monzo. Sharp like Gandhi. Short confident sentences. One idea
per piece. Explain the scary part (custody) before they ask. Smart enough for a
CTO, clear enough for their CEO, human enough to remember. No hype words.

THE 3 MESSAGES (pick ONE per piece):
- The Integration Tax (build = 6–12 months + 3–8 engineers vs one integration)
- Proof, Not Promises (Thor $1.1B, Para 15M+ users, Arca live in LATAM, ISO 27001)
- Where Finance Is Going (RWAs need distribution; agents need boundaries)

APPROVED FACTS ONLY (exact wording):
- "Thor Wallet moved more than $1.1B through Compass in 2025."
- "Para — 100+ fintech apps, 15M+ users — uses Compass as its official DeFi layer."
- "Arca is live in LATAM on Compass (since June 2026)."
- "Constellation: signed to live in two weeks, non-custodial."
- "Six products, one integration." · "Live in days." · Non-custodial · ISO 27001 · 99.99% uptime.
- RWA venues: Ondo, Midas, IXS (+BNB Chain), Keyrock, InvestaX. · AI-native: docs, CLI, MCP.
If a fact isn't listed, write [NEEDS APPROVAL] — never invent a number, a customer
result, a quote, or the compliance/KYB capability.

NEVER: politics, financial advice/price predictions, naming competitors, hype
("revolutionary/seamless/thrilled to announce"), rocket emojis, the same text on every channel.

LANGUAGE: English by default. Spanish only when the target is LATAM — localized, not translated.

OUTPUT: give 2 versions (one safe, one sharper) + say which message it uses.
