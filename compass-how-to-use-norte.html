Compass Labs · How to use Norte 🧭

Just talk to Norte.

Norte is our AI marketing co-pilot. You don’t need to write, design, or know anything technical. You ask — it makes it on-brand.

The whole trick: tell Norte what you want, in plain words. “Write me a post about our BNB launch.” · “Make an image that says X.” · “Build the campaign for our new customer.” That’s it.

What to say to get what you want

Copy the phrasing. If Norte needs more, it’ll ask you — that’s normal.

You want…Just say…
A social post“write me a post about [what happened], for LinkedIn”
An image / billboard“make me an image that says [your line]”
A whole campaign“build the campaign for [the moment]” → posts, emails, images, plan
An email“draft an email to [who] about [what]”
Fix a rough draft“make this sound like Compass” (paste your text)

Two ways to use Norte

Same Norte, two “places.” Pick based on whether you need words or real files.

Quick text · anywhere in Claude

Use the skills in the Claude app or web

Type /post, /email, etc. (or just describe it). You get finished, on-brand words in the chat. Copy, paste, publish. Perfect for a quick post or email.

Real files · in Cowork

Use Norte in Cowork

Ask the same things and you get actual files — image PNGs, a full campaign folder, everything ready to download. No design or technical skills needed. This is where images and full campaigns come from.

Simple rule: need words → anywhere. Need image files or a full campaign package → Cowork.

The 3 rules that never change

1
Norte writes. A human always posts. Nothing publishes automatically — on purpose, so someone reviews first.
2
Only approved facts. If Norte writes [NEEDS APPROVAL], that number or claim must be confirmed before it goes out.
3
Same voice, same message, every time. The Compass voice, the 3 core messages, and the guardrails are already baked into every skill. You just ask.
