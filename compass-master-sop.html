The #1 thing to understand: you never write content from scratch, and you never have to be "good at marketing." You tell Norte what happened in plain words. Norte writes the post. You post it. That's it.

How to use Norte, the 2 ways

It's the same Norte in two "places." Pick based on whether you need words or real files. This is the one thing people get confused by, so here it is, plainly.

✍️ QUICK TEXT · anywhere in Claude

Type /post, /email, etc. (or just describe it) in the Claude app or web. You get finished, on-brand words in the chat. Copy, paste, publish. Perfect for a quick post or email.

📦 REAL FILES · in Cowork

Ask the same things in Cowork and you get actual files, image PNGs, a full campaign folder, everything ready to download. No design or technical skills needed. This is where images and full campaigns come from.

Simple rule: need words → use it anywhere. Need image files or a whole campaign package → use Cowork.
The one that trips people up: /campaign does NOT hand you a folder of finished images by itself. It writes everything (blog, posts, billboard lines, emails, plan); turning the billboard lines into real PNGs is one extra step, in Canva, or by running the campaign in Cowork. No skill does that automatically. Not a bug.
What to say to get what you want

A post → "write me a post about [what happened], for LinkedIn"

An image → "make me an image that says [your line]"

A campaign → "build the campaign for [the moment]"

An email → "draft an email to [who] about [what]"

Fix a draft → paste it, "make this sound like Compass"

If Norte needs more, it asks. That's normal.

1 · The basics

What is "the machine"?

It's just a simple, repeatable way of turning things that happen at Compass into content that brings us customers. It has two parts:

🕐 THE CLOCK

Every week has the same rhythm (Monday decide, mid-week post, Friday follow up). So we're never silent, even in a slow week.

⚡ THE TRIGGERS

When something real happens (we shipped X, we won Y, a customer said Z), that becomes a post. You "flag" it and Norte writes it.

That's the whole machine. Nothing more complicated than that.

2 · What is Norte?

Norte is an AI assistant that already knows how Compass talks, what facts we're allowed to say, and our 3 core messages. You open Claude (the AI app), and Norte is there to write anything you need, a post, an email, a message to a prospect.

YOU TYPE"Norte, write a LinkedIn post, we just went live with IXS on BNB Chain."
NORTE GIVES YOUA finished post in Compass's voice, with the right facts, ready to paste. (Usually 2 versions, pick one.)
Everyone has Norte. Eli, Aidar, Funa, Bezkat, Gin, each in their own Claude. So anyone can make an on-brand post in 30 seconds without asking anyone.

How to get Norte set up in your Claude: see the "How to Use Norte" doc, you paste one prompt, once, and you're done.

3 · The words we use (so nothing's confusing)

Signal

A one-line note about something that happened. Ex: "WON: Roqqu signed." You drop these in Slack.

Post

Content you publish on X or LinkedIn. Norte writes it; you post it.

Franchise

A recurring "show" we post regularly (ex: "Objection of the Week"). A format, so you never start from zero.

Campaign

A bigger push over 2, 3 weeks around one moment (like the Earn launch). Many posts, one theme.

Claims library

The list of facts we're allowed to say, worded exactly (ex: "Thor Wallet is a customer"). Norte only uses these.

UTM

A bit of text we add to a link so we can see where a click came from. Norte makes these for you.

Attio

Our CRM, the list of companies we talk to. It's where we track who's interested.

Activate

Sending a personal message to a specific company so a campaign turns into a real conversation.

2 · Doing it

4 · One-time setup (do once, ~1 hour)

  1. Slack channel. Create #marketing-signals, add everyone, and pin the message below. (Gin does this.)
  2. Everyone installs Norte. Each person pastes the Norte prompt into their own Claude, once. (From the "How to Use Norte" doc.)
  3. A tracking sheet. One Google Sheet: columns Date · Channel · Post · Link · Result. This is where we jot what we published.
The pinned Slack message, paste this📣 Marketing signals go here. One line, 10 seconds. Norte turns it into content. Format: [TYPE] what happened (+ company/link) • SHIPPED: RWA latency now under X ms • WON: IXS + BNB Chain venue live 🎉 • OBJECTION: [Littio] says they need Solana • CUSTOMER: quote from Arca call, "…" • IDEA: blog on build-vs-buy math? When in doubt, drop it. Better a messy note than a missed signal.

Not needed to start: Buffer (later), fancy Attio fields (later). You can run everything with Slack + Claude + your normal accounts.

2 · Doing it

5 · How to flag a signal (10 seconds)

  1. Something happened in your world? Open #marketing-signals in Slack.
  2. Type one line: the TYPE + what happened. Ex: OBJECTION: Belo asked about Solana. Hit enter. Done.
That is the entire job. You don't write a post. You don't format anything. Norte reads these later and turns the good ones into content.

How does a signal become a post? Gin (or you) copies the signal and asks Norte to write it (next section). Today that's a human step, Slack collects, Norte writes, a human posts. Simple and real.

6 · How to make a post with Norte

  1. Open Claude (with Norte set up).
  2. Type what you want in plain words. Ex: "Norte, write me an X post, we shipped faster RWA settlement."
  3. Norte gives you 2 versions. Read them. Pick one. Change a word if you want.
  4. Check: does it use a NEW number that's not in the claims library? If yes → ping Aidar for a 👍 first. If it's an approved fact, you're good.
  5. Now go post it (next sections tell you how, per channel).
YOU"Norte, a customer asked if we hold their funds. Give me a short post answering it."
NORTE"Do you hold the funds? No. Users sign in their own wallets. Compass never touches the assets…", ready to post or turn into a 30-sec video.
2 · Doing it · publishing

7 · Post it on X (Twitter)

  1. Copy the post Norte gave you.
  2. Go to X → click "Post" → paste.
  3. If there's a link, add the UTM (see §14). For a thread, paste part 1, then reply with part 2, etc.
  4. Publish. Ask teammates to reshare in the first hour.
  5. Write it in the tracking sheet (date, "X", the link).
X = the sharpest, shortest version. One idea. Never "excited to announce."
2 · Doing it · publishing

8 · Post it on LinkedIn

  1. Copy Norte's LinkedIn version (a bit longer, with one clear CTA).
  2. Company post: go to the Compass LinkedIn page → "Start a post" → paste. Personal post: post from your own profile.
  3. Put the link in the FIRST comment, not the post body (LinkedIn shows link-posts to fewer people). Add the UTM.
  4. Everyone reshares/comments in the first hour, it helps a lot.
  5. Log it in the sheet.
2 · Doing it · publishing

9 · Send an email (Mailchimp)

  1. Ask Norte for the email (subject + short, personal body).
  2. Mailchimp → Campaigns → Create → Email → Regular.
  3. To: your audience, filtered by the right Tag (ex: LATAM). This sends it only to the right people.
  4. Paste subject + body. Add utm_source=mailchimp to any link.
  5. Send a test to yourself. Then Send.
  6. After 2 days, note replies (replies matter more than opens).
Mailchimp only for: the segmented email + the monthly newsletter. Personal 1-to-1 messages go from Gmail, not Mailchimp, they work much better sent by a human.
2 · Doing it · publishing

10 · Send a DM to a company (this is how we get meetings)

  1. Ask Norte: "Write me an opener for [company], they're a stablecoin app in Colombia interested in yield." (Or for your accounts, Gin's Target List already has these.)
  2. Norte gives you 3 sentences: their situation → the relevant thing → one easy question.
  3. Change [name] + one real detail so it feels personal.
  4. Send it from LinkedIn DM or Gmail.
  5. In Attio, open that company → add a note about it → move the stage if it advanced.
Never a mass blast. Each message mentions something real about them. That's why people reply.

11 · Make a video

Two kinds. In both, Norte writes the script, you (or a tool) just record it.

PRODUCT VIDEO · Bezkat

1. Bezkat tells Norte the product truth (10-min brain-dump). 2. Norte writes the script (hook first, one idea). 3. Bezkat records the product + edits. 4. Gin approves.

TALKING-HEAD · later

For explainers/objections. Norte writes the script → HeyGen (AI avatar) generates it, or someone records it on a phone (raw is fine).

The script always comes from Norte. That's what fixes videos that "explain like it's obvious" and don't land.

Plan first, build after (the two phases)

The most important rule of the week: you approve the plan as text before anything heavy gets made.

  1. Phase 1, plan the words. Monday, Norte fills the canvas in #norte-warroom with the whole week: every post, thread, email and blog, by day and by channel. Gin and Eli review it right there, move and cut, and lock it. It is live in Slack instantly, no upload needed, so Eli in London sees it the moment it runs. Anything with numbers or a technical claim goes to Aidar before it locks.
  2. Phase 2, build the visuals. Only once the week is locked, Gin says "go" and Norte builds the billboards and the rendered week HTML for the approved pieces. Gin publishes that to the site or Drive whenever she wants. Because it is already approved, no asset is wasted.
Where it lives: the canvas in #norte-warroom is the living team view (instant, editable, no upload, this is what Eli reads). The billboards and rendered HTML are the polished extra, published after the plan is locked to one fixed link on the site (compass-week-EVERYTHING.html) or to Drive. Plan and approve in the canvas, then build. Nobody waits on an upload to see the plan.
Your one meeting: the 30-min Monday. Everything else is you + Norte, whenever it fits.

13 · Run a campaign (a 2, 3 week push)

  1. Gin + Eli decide it's worth a campaign (on Monday).
  2. Ask Norte: "Build the full campaign for [Earn / LATAM]." → you get all the posts, the email, the video script, the account list.
  3. Eli approves the angle; Aidar clears any new claim.
  4. Post them in order across the week (Gin's GO Battle Plan has the exact day-by-day).
  5. Send personal DMs to the named accounts.
  6. After 1 week and 1 month, ask Norte to review what worked. Reuse the winners.

14 · Measure it (light, not scary)

  1. UTM on links: before sharing a link, add ?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN. Not sure? Ask Norte: "give me the UTM link."
  2. In Attio: after a call or reply, add a note + move the stage. That's how we prove marketing created pipeline.
  3. The one number that matters: qualified conversations/meetings marketing created, not likes.
You don't need to fill lots of data. A note + a stage move after a call is enough.

15 · The rules (so nothing wrong goes out)

✅ Ships freely

Uses the approved sentence + approved facts + a known franchise. Just post it.

⚠️ Needs a quick 👍

A NEW technical number → Aidar. A named customer/partner → the relationship owner.

Never: invent a number, use an unapproved fact, make a price prediction, name a competitor, use hype words ("revolutionary", "thrilled to announce", 🚀), or talk politics. If unsure, Norte writes [NEEDS APPROVAL] and you check first.
