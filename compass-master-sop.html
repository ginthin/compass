Read this first

The whole system in 5 lines.

  1. Signals come in — the team drops one-line events in a Slack channel.
  2. Norte drafts — the AI turns signals into posts, emails, video scripts, openers.
  3. Gin approves — you check voice; CTO 👍 any new technical claim.
  4. We publish + activate — post on X/LinkedIn, send the email, DM named accounts.
  5. We measure — everything tagged in Attio; review at 7 and 30 days.
The golden rule: nobody writes from a blank page. You either follow the weekly rhythm (§2) or react to a trigger (§1). Norte does the writing. You decide, approve, and send.
0 · Setup · one-time · ~2–3 hours

Get the tools in order (do this once).

0.1 · Slack signals channel

  1. Create a channel #marketing-signals. Add everyone (Eli, Aidar, Funa, Commercial, you). Gin
  2. Pin the message below so everyone knows the format.
Pinned message — paste this in the channel📣 This is where we drop marketing signals. One line, 10 seconds, no need to write nicely — Norte turns it into content. Format: [TYPE] what happened (+ company/link if relevant) Examples: • SHIPPED: RWA latency now under X ms • WON: IXS venue live 🎉 • OBJECTION: [Littio] says they need Solana • CUSTOMER: quote from Arca call — "…" • MARKET: competitor X launched Y — react? • IDEA: blog on build-vs-buy math? When in doubt, drop it. Better a messy note than a missed signal.

0.2 · Attio (measurable)

  1. Add 5 fields to Companies: Segment Country Product interest Source campaign Deal value. Gin
  2. Create list [Gin] LATAM sales; add your 5 LATAM targets.

0.3 · Mailchimp

  1. One Audience: Compass Contacts. Import from Attio (CSV).
  2. Create Tags: LATAM Africa Stablecoin-app Wallet Signup. Tag as you import.

0.4 · Tracking sheet + claims library

  1. One Google Sheet "Launch tracker": Date · Channel · Asset · Link(UTM) · Result.
  2. Keep the Claims Library open in a tab (approved facts, one click away).
Buffer = later. Until then you post by hand. Full click-steps for Attio/Mailchimp are in the Setup + Launch Runbook.
1 · Flagging & signals · the "how" of turning events into content

From a Slack line to a published post.

This is the answer to "how do I flag it?" and "how does it become a post?" — spelled out.

For the team — how to flag (10 seconds)

  1. Something happened in your world? Open #marketing-signals.
  2. Type one line in the format (TYPE + what happened). Hit enter. Done. anyone
That's the whole job. You don't write a post, you don't format anything. "OBJECTION: Belo asked about Solana" is a perfect flag. Norte takes it from there.

For Gin — how the signal becomes content (the Friday sweep)

  1. Friday, 20 min: open #marketing-signals and skim the week's flags. Also glance at Attio (new stages, notes) + the changelog. Gin
  2. Copy the signals into one message to Norte: "Here are this week's signals: [paste]. Which deserve content? Draft the strongest 3." Norte
  3. Norte gives you drafts + tells you which angle/franchise each is. You skim, pick, adjust.
  4. The chosen ones go into next week's plan (see §2 Monday).
Honest note: today this is collect → Norte drafts → you approve — not a bot auto-posting from Slack. That's fine; it's simple and works day one. Later we can connect Slack + Attio directly to automate the collection.

3 · Publish · X (Twitter)

How to post on X.

  1. Ask Norte: "Draft the X post/thread for [signal/topic]." You get 2 versions.
  2. Pick one, tweak if you want. Check it against the claims library (§12).
  3. Go to X → new post → paste. For a thread, paste each part as a reply to the previous. Gin
  4. If there's a link, add the UTM (§8). Post.
  5. Log it in the tracking sheet (date, "X", link). Later: reply to comments.
Sharpest tension goes on X. One idea. No "excited to announce." Founders (Eli) reshare from their accounts.
4 · Publish · LinkedIn

How to post on LinkedIn (company + personal).

  1. Ask Norte for the LinkedIn version (buyer outcome + proof + one CTA).
  2. Company post → Compass LinkedIn page → Start a post → paste. Personal → each founder posts from their own profile (their voice, never a copy of the company post).
  3. Put the link in the FIRST comment (not the post body) — LinkedIn shows posts-with-links to fewer people. Add UTM.
  4. Everyone on the team reshares/comments in the first hour (helps reach).
  5. Log it.
5 · Publish · Blog

How to publish a blog.

  1. Ask Norte for the full blog (thesis title, problem, build-vs-buy, objections, CTA). Norte
  2. CTO checks any technical claim (§12).
  3. Paste into the website blog (compasslabs.ai/blog admin). Add a clear title + one image/diagram if you have it.
  4. Publish → grab the URL → add UTM for when you share it.
  5. Share the blog link from LinkedIn + X + the email.
The blog is the "definitive piece" — it sells through usefulness. A CTO should be able to forward it to their CEO.
6 · Publish · Mailchimp (email)

How to send a campaign email.

  1. Ask Norte for the email (subject + plain-text body). Keep it personal, not a designed template.
  2. Mailchimp → Campaigns → Create → Email → Regular.
  3. To: your Audience, filtered by the right Tags (e.g. LATAM + Stablecoin-app). This is how you send only to the right people. Gin
  4. Paste subject + body. Add the UTM utm_source=mailchimp to any link.
  5. Send a test to yourself first. Then Send (or Schedule).
  6. Log opens/replies after 48h. Replies > opens — a reply means a real conversation.
Mailchimp is only for: the segmented campaign email, the monthly newsletter, and (later) automated signup sequences. Personal 1:1 outreach goes from Gmail, not Mailchimp — it converts far better.
7 · Outreach · DMs & personal email

How to activate a named account (this is where pipeline is made).

  1. Open the account in Attio — read its notes/history (or ask Norte: "research [company] + write me an opener").
  2. Norte drafts a 3-sentence opener: their context → the relevant new thing → one easy question.
  3. Personalize [name] + one real detail. Send from Gmail or LinkedIn DM. Gin/Funa/Commercial
  4. Log it in Attio: add a note, set Source campaign, move the stage if it advances.
Never a blast. Each DM references something real about them. That's why they reply.
8 · Tracking · UTMs

How to tag links so we can measure.

A UTM is just text you add to a link so we can see where a click came from. Add it to every link you share.

The patternYOUR-LINK?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN utm_source = x | linkedin | mailchimp | newsletter utm_campaign = the campaign code, e.g. 202608-earn-latam Examples: compasslabs.ai/blog/earn?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=202608-earn-latam compasslabs.ai/demo?utm_source=mailchimp&utm_campaign=202608-earn-latam
  1. Before sharing a link, add ?utm_source=…&utm_campaign=….
  2. Paste the tagged link in the tracking sheet. Done.
Not sure? Ask Norte "give me the UTM link for [page] on [channel] for [campaign]" and paste the result.
9 · Measure · Log it in Attio

How to keep Attio current (light, not heavy).

  1. After any call or reply: open the company in Attio → add a note with the outcome + any objection (prefix objections with objection:). whoever had the call
  2. Move the stage if it advanced (Prospecting → Meeting → etc.).
  3. Set Source campaign so we know what marketing touched it.
Why it matters: Attio is how we prove "marketing created/advanced this pipeline" — the number the fundraise needs. Two minutes after a call keeps it true.
10 · Run a campaign · end to end

How to launch a full campaign (2–3 weeks).

  1. Trigger: a signal scores big on Monday (or a planned launch). Max one campaign at a time. Gin+Eli
  2. Brief: ask Norte "build the campaign brief + all channels for [topic/product/segment]." You get the brief + X + LinkedIn + blog + email + video script + billboards + the account list.
  3. Approve: Eli signs the angle; CTO clears claims (§12).
  4. Teaser (optional): drop a teaser 2–3 days before to build anticipation.
  5. Launch week: follow the sequence — Day 1–2 go loud (X + video + billboards), Day 3 go deep (blog + LinkedIn), Day 4 email, Day 5–10 personal DMs to named accounts.
  6. Activate: 10–15 named accounts get a personal DM (§7), logged in Attio.
  7. Review: +7 days (which angle got replies) and +30 days (meetings/pipeline). Ask Norte to pull it.
  8. Reuse: ask Norte to turn winners into sales slides + investor-update lines.
The full worked example ("Put the Money to Work") is in the flagship campaign doc + the launch runbook. This is the repeatable procedure for any future campaign.
11 · Signup lifecycle · the fast growth win

How to work API signups (simple, human-first).

  1. Aidar sends the signup export (email, date, activation events). Aidar
  2. Ask Norte: "score these signups + give me the top 20–30 with a personal opener each." Norte
  3. For each warm one, send the personal email from Gmail. Log in Attio (Source = api-signup). Gin
  4. The segments: Explorer (solo dev — help, don't sell) · Builder (eng — remove friction) · Buyer (real company — route to a call) · Ghost (never activated — one honest question: "what got in the way?").
  5. Cap: max ~4 emails in 3 weeks, each genuinely useful. Someone replies → a human takes over.
  6. Once a sequence works by hand, automate it in Mailchimp (later).
Watch the special trigger: heavy API usage but never contacts us + never pays → reach out ("is pricing in the way?"). Could reveal a self-serve revenue line.
12 · Approvals & claims · so nothing wrong ships

How to approve (fast) and keep claims safe.

  1. Ships with no approval: uses the approved sentence + approved claims + a known franchise + passes the guardrails. Just post it.
  2. Needs a quick gate: a NEW technical number/claim → CTO 👍 in Slack (~2 min). A named customer/partner → relationship owner clears it.
  3. Needs a real decision: new positioning, sensitive customer content, or anything about compliance/KYB → Eli + Gin (+ CTO).
  4. If unsure of a fact: Norte writes [NEEDS APPROVAL] — never guesses. You get it confirmed, then it's added to the claims library.
The hard line: no claim ships unless it's in the claims library. No invented metrics, no customer results without written approval, no compliance/KYB as a shipped feature. When in doubt, don't.

Reference · glossary (plain words)

What the terms mean.

Signal — a one-line event you drop in Slack that can become content.
Franchise — a recurring "show" (e.g. Objection of the Week). Repeatable format so nobody faces a blank page.
Campaign — a 2–3 week multi-channel push around one big moment.
Angle — one of our 3 arguments (Integration Tax · Proof · Where Finance Is Going). Every piece uses one.
UTM — text added to a link so we can see where a click came from.
Claims library — the list of approved facts with exact wording. The only claims we're allowed to make.
Activation — sending a personal DM to a named account so a campaign becomes pipeline.
North star — qualified opportunities created or advanced by marketing. The one number that matters.
