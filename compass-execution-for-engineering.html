In 30 seconds · what we're building

A repeatable commercial engine, the thing the raise scales.

The fundraise use-of-funds is marketing + sales. So we're not "doing more content", we're building a machine that turns real company signals (shipped features, customer news, partnerships, objections) into narratives → campaigns → pipeline, measured in the CRM. AI does the production; humans do judgment and taste. We validate it works before decks go out (early September), so investors fund a proven motion.

The one thing to understand: the whole system is designed to run on ~45 minutes of human decision-time a week + AI production. Engineering's slice of that is ~30 minutes: keep our claims honest, and occasionally explain something on camera-adjacent. That's the footprint.

The machine · the loop

Capture → Produce → Approve → Distribute → Measure.

Signals come in from the tools we already use (Attio, Slack, Gmail, Calendar, changelog). AI scores them, drafts everything, and a human approves. It ships, gets activated on named accounts, and everything is measured in Attio with campaign tags. Winners get reused into sales + investor material.

AUTOMATED

Capture & Produce

AI reads the signals, ranks them, and drafts the posts, emails, video scripts, UTMs and account lists. No human types first drafts.

HUMAN GATE

Approve

Gin approves voice + priority. CTO clears any new technical claim (async, ~2 min). Partner/customer mentions cleared by the relationship owner. Most content ships on the rules, no gate.

AUTOMATED

Distribute & Measure

Buffer schedules, Attio tracks source_campaign, reports auto-generate at 7 and 30 days. Winners get reused.

Aidar, this part is for you · engineering's role

Two real jobs: the product videos, and keeping the numbers honest.

Engineering is not a content team. But you own two things nobody else can, and you flag things when it's your turn. Here's the whole footprint:

JOB 1 · Bezkat

🎬 Product videos

Bezkat keeps making the product videos, that stays. What changes: the script comes from Norte. Bezkat gives Norte the substance (a 10-min "what we built, why it's hard"), Norte writes the outcome-first script, Bezkat produces it. Fixes why videos don't land today.

Bezkat produces · Norte scripts · same effort, better videos
JOB 2 · CTO

🔢 Keep the numbers honest

You're the guardrail. When a post uses a NEW technical number or claim, you 👍 it (or correct it) in Slack. Everything else ships on the approved claims library, so this is rare. Precision is the product for a non-custodial company.

~15 min/week, async, no meetings
JOB 3 · everyone in eng

⚡ Flag + do your turn

When something ships or breaks-and-fixed, drop a line in #marketing-signals. And when it's your turn to explain something (a video, a technical post), you have Norte, ask it, it writes it. Plus: the signup data export (this week, unblocks a lot).

A Slack line when it happens + the signup export once
Why the Bezkat + Norte split matters (the honest bit): our current videos don't perform because they're made by whoever knows the product best, so they explain it as if it's obvious, feature-first, no hook. The fix isn't "engineers get good at marketing." It's: Bezkat brings the truth, Norte brings the story, Bezkat shoots it. He just answers "what did we build, why is it hard, what breaks without it?", Norte turns that into a script that actually lands.

The safeguards · so nothing embarrassing ships

How we stay accurate and on-brand.

The claims library

Every approved number, worded exactly. Anything not on it = [NEEDS APPROVAL] → comes to you before it ships. No unverified metrics, no customer financials, and nothing about compliance/KYB as a shipped feature (it's a thesis, treated as vision only).

Human approval + guardrails

Gin approves everything for voice; a NOT-list is baked into the AI (no hype, no politics, no price predictions, no naming competitors). Videos are template-based, not uncanny avatars, and we launch with no-face videos until any avatar passes a quality test.
