  • Claims library v1 — every approved number + exact wording, one page GinCTO checks
  • Guardrails (the NOT-list) written + dropped into the AI prompt Gin
  • Attio upgrade — 5 fields (segment/country/product/campaign/value) + [Gin] LATAM + [Funa] lists Gin
  • Connect the sources — Attio, Slack, Gmail, Calendar into the flow GinAI
  • 🔴 Signup export — get the data (CTO), Claude enriches + scores everyone CTOAI
  • Hit the top 20–30 by hand — personal DMs referencing their context GinFuna/Sales
  • Eli keeps her current cadence (no gap) + drop the teaser end of week EliGin

UNBLOCK FIRST

  • Ask CTO for signup data access — day 1
  • Assign the Arca approval owner — day 1
  • Get Buffer + Mailchimp admin

TOOLS

Don't wait — start Monday morning

The first 5 things you do, in order.

1

Message the CTO for signup data access + name the Arca approval owner. These two unblock everything — send both before your coffee's cold.

2

Lock the canonical sentence with Eli (30 min) and paste it + the claims list into one doc. That doc becomes the AI prompt.

3

Ask Claude to pull & score the signup export (once you have it) and the Attio accounts — get the top 20–30 target list.

4

Send 10 personal DMs to the warmest names on that list. Real context, one question each. Pipeline starts today, not week 3.

5

Post one thing — a Backlog Files line or a sharp take. The feed goes live this week, even before the engine is polished.

Everything else follows the plan above. But momentum starts with these five — today.

What "perfectly oiled" looks like at the end

By early September, this is running without heroics.

CAPTURE → PRODUCE

Signals flow in from Attio/Slack/Calendar automatically. Every Monday, Claude hands you a ranked digest and drafts the week. You choose and approve — 45 min total.

Two publish days, videos on a template, named accounts getting personal DMs, signup leads nurtured on triggers. The feed is always alive; sales always has warm context.

MEASURE → REUSE → RAISE

Every campaign tagged, every result in Attio, the traction story assembling itself. New hires inherit a proven machine. The round scales a system that already works.
