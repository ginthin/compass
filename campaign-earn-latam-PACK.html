ELI · LinkedInWhere Finance Is Going
Most fintechs in LATAM think offering yield means becoming a custodian. It doesn’t. That belief is the single most expensive mistake I see. Here’s what I’ve learned watching teams try: the hard part was never the yield. It was the rails underneath — custody, compliance, the audit, the months.
