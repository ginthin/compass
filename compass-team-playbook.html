Why we're doing this

We're more capable than our competitors, and harder to understand.

That gap is the most expensive thing at Compass. We lose deals before the comparison even happens, because people don't get what we do fast enough. Confusion is our #1 competitor. The fix isn't "post more", it's a machine that makes Compass instantly legible, creates demand, and turns it into pipeline. And the timing is everything:

THE STAKES

The raise funds this

Use-of-funds is marketing + sales. Investors back a proven motion. So we build the engine and prove it works before decks go out (~early September).

THE BET

Do it now, small

We validate the whole system with a tiny team + AI in 5 weeks. Then the round scales a machine that already works, not a wish.

THE VISION

Bigger than an API

We're the layer to launch and operate financial products onchain. "Everyone else sells a piece. We make you able to operate." That's the story we go tell.

How the machine works · in one minute

Signals in → AI produces → we approve → it ships → we measure.

Everything you already do generates signals, a shipped feature, a customer win, a partnership, an objection on a call. Those flow into the machine. AI drafts the content, videos and outreach. A human approves. It ships across channels, lands on named accounts, and gets measured in Attio. Winners get reused into sales and the investor deck.

AI DOES

The drafts, the videos, the scoring, the account lists, the reports. The boring, heavy lifting. Nobody stares at a blank page.

WE DO

The judgment, the relationships, the calls, the taste. We decide what matters and approve it. ~45 min of human decision-time a week, total.

THE RULE

Everyone sells. We all have a voice and a lane. The company says "what happened + why buyers care"; each of us says "what we learned + what the market gets wrong."

The unlock, everyone has Norte 🧭: you each get Norte (our AI) in your own Claude. Need a post, an opener, a video script? Just ask it, "write me a post about X", and it comes back on-brand, with the right facts, in 30 seconds. Content no longer waits on Gin. Gin runs the official accounts + big campaigns; for your own posts and outreach, you're self-serve. (Full how-to: "How to Use Norte".)

The recurring shows, open to everyone

The franchises, with an example of each.

A franchise is a named series on a fixed day. The host is just who usually starts it, not a fence, and topics are diverse. Anyone can front any of them. Here is each one with a real example episode, so you see what it looks like.

Objection of the Week
USUAL HOST: SALES & COMMERCIAL (OPEN TO ALL) · MONDAY
"Coinbase pays about 3.25% with no ops burden, why take DeFi risk for 3.5%?" Fair question. Here is what flips it. Your CEX yield is not magic, it routes into Morpho anyway. So you are already taking the DeFi risk, just paying a middleman to sit in front of it. Do it yourself, non-custodial, and you cut the intermediary, not add risk.
What we shipped
USUAL HOST: ENGINEERING, AIDAR + BEZKAT (OPEN TO ALL) · MIDWEEK · BEZKAT CAN FRONT ON CAMERA
We shipped something small that kills a big friction. Swap-and-deposit: any stablecoin comes in, we swap it and deposit it into a yield vault, in a single signature. No juggling tokens, no five step flow. One signature, funds earning.
Customer wins and proof
USUAL HOST: SALES & COMMERCIAL (OPEN TO ALL) · WHEN THERE IS A WIN
Arca shipped dollar-yield to their users in weeks, non-custodial, live in LATAM. The 8 months their CTO quoted became one integration. Proof, not promises.
On the ground (region)
USUAL HOST: FUNA + REGIONAL COMMERCIAL (OPEN TO ALL) · WEEKLY
In Africa, apps are already selling anti-devaluation savings and 7.5% APY. The demand is here, loud, in every market at once. What they are missing is the layer that lets them ship it without becoming a DeFi company. That is the gap Compass fills, market by market.
Founder take
USUAL HOST: ELI (ANY FOUNDER VOICE) · FRIDAY
Here is what I think most fintechs get wrong. They treat holding stablecoins as the finish line. It is the starting line. For teams already integrated, adding earn, borrow, trade is a parameter. Not a project, not a security review, not a quarter. Every fintech is becoming a wallet. The ones that win will let the money actually work.
Where finance is going
USUAL HOST: ANYONE, FED FROM #market-news · ONGOING
$35 trillion moved on-chain last year and stablecoins stopped being a crypto category. Everyone now holds digital dollars, and almost nobody can do anything with them. That gap, between the money being onchain and anyone being able to use it, is the biggest opportunity in fintech right now.
The host is just who usually starts it. Funa can drop an objection, Bezkat a market take, sales the build story. You drop the raw signal into #marketing-signals, Norte writes the episode, and it maps back to the three messages: the integration tax, proof not promises, and where finance is going.
