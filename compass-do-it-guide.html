Week 1 · the build, step by step (you + me)

The video plan · de-risked, because it stresses you out (fair)

Don't let video block the launch. Phase it.

Your instinct is right: HeyGen means a subscription, building avatars, testing quality, Spanish voice — a whole thing. So we don't put it on the critical path. Video comes in three phases, and Phase 0 needs zero avatars.

PHASE 0 · launch with this

No-face videos

Screen recording of the product/Studio + captions + proof cards + text, cut in CapCut (free). No avatar, no HeyGen, no one on camera. This carries the Earn campaign completely. ~1 hr per video once you have a template.

This is enough to launch. Fully.

PHASE 1 · test in parallel

HeyGen trial

Get the trial/cheapest plan. Make ONE avatar, generate ONE 30-sec clip in EN and ES. Watch it critically: does the Spanish sound human? Is it uncanny? Decide from the test, not the hype. Don't roll it out until it passes.

Budget a small monthly cap only if the test passes.

PHASE 2 · scale if it works

Avatars + real record

If HeyGen passes: use avatars for objection/explainer videos. For trust (Eli, customers): real record — a clipped Zoom or phone video, raw is fine. The crafted product film comes here too.

Weeks 2–4, not Week 1.

The rule that removes the stress: Phase 0 is the only thing you need before launch. A campaign with clean no-face videos + strong billboards + great copy launches perfectly. HeyGen is an upgrade you test calmly in parallel — if the avatars are bad, we simply don't use them and lose nothing. Video never blocks the raise timeline.
