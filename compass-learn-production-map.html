Publishing cadence · the important one

Produce in batches. Publish on a drip.

We never ship 10 videos in a day. We record and render in one weekly block, then release slowly, so each video gets its moment and its social push.

to the hub

2 lessons a week

Two hub videos published per week, on set days (for example Tuesday and Thursday). The full library lands over about 8 weeks, not at once.

to social

2 to 3 shorts a week

101 clips and product takes trickle out through Typefully. Never more than one a day.

the rule

Max about 2 a day

If a day would ship more, it moves to the next slot. Steady beats a dump.

Why it matters: a video dumped with nine others gets no attention and no social juice. A drip means every asset gets a launch, the feed stays alive all week, and the team is never crushed. Production is batched, publishing is paced.
Is it too much?

No, because it is one library, reused.

It looks like a lot of videos, but a small team ships it, for three reasons. Most are animated or avatar, so no filming. Every asset is made once and lands in many places (hub, campaign, social, API), so one video does four jobs. And it is produced in one weekly block, not scattered across the week. The volume is real, the effort is not.
