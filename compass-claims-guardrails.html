🛑 The guardrails — what Compass never says or does

The lines we don't cross. Ever.

No politics / elections / partisan takes — any country. We sell everywhere; we take no side.
No religion or social wedge issues. Not our lane.
No unverified claims or numbers. Approved list only, or [NEEDS APPROVAL].
No invented customer quotes or results. Written customer approval always.
No financial advice or price predictions. We're infrastructure, not an advisor.
No attacking named competitors. Beat alternatives with receipts, never name-and-shame.
No hype words: "revolutionary", "seamless", "best-in-class", "the future of finance", "thrilled to announce", 🚀. They make us invisible.
No fake urgency / engagement bait ("🚨", "you won't believe", "tag a founder").
No overclaiming custody / security. Be precise: non-custodial = users hold keys.
No memes that punch down / stereotypes. Wit, never cheap shots.
No confidential pipeline in public. No in-negotiation names without sign-off.
No same text across every channel. One idea, adapted per channel.
