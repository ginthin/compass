The Onchain Sea.

A vast glowing ocean where value moves in rivers of light, trillions of it. It should be paradise, but a grey Fog hides the islands, and travelers cannot reach the products they came for. The North Star above is the direction everything is sailing toward. Nova is the only one who can read it.

The places, up close

What the Sea actually looks like.

Three establishing shots. This is the set an episode is staged in, so the same places recur video after video and the world starts to feel real.

Newcomer's Harbor

where every episode opens

Warm lamplit docks at the edge of the Fog. Little boats tied up, sails in Compass colours, travelers milling about with no idea which way is out. This is the first place anyone sees, and it is deliberately cozy — the world is not scary, it is just unmapped. Nova hangs above the pier, needle already pointing.

The Fog

the villain, and the middle of every episode

A grey wall of confusion — jargon, twelve tabs, "build it yourself." It has no face and never speaks; it just sits between a traveler and the thing they wanted. Nova flies into it and her star cuts a beam through, and on the far side one island is suddenly visible. That beam is the money shot of the whole series.

An island — Earn Isle

the arrival, how all six islands are staged

Past the Fog the light changes: warm, open, gold. Every island follows the same recipe — a round green-lavender landmass, one gold landmark on top that says what the island is for (a lantern for yield, a vault door for Loan Cove, a lighthouse for Studio), a small dock, and a traveler's boat pulling in. Swap the landmark and you have the next island without redesigning anything.
