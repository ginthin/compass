Compass Labs · Nova 🌟 · design & rig brief

Bring Nova to life.

A brief for an illustrator/animator or small studio. We have the character, the world and the story. We need the beautiful, high-quality art and a reusable rig, so we can produce Nova videos forever without redrawing.

The mission

What this is.

Compass Learn is our education engine, hosted by Nova, a little star-compass mascot who guides people through onchain finance. She lives in a world called the Onchain Sea. We publish a lot of short videos, so Nova must be designed once, beautifully, and rigged so anyone on the team can produce new episodes fast and consistent. Full character, world and story bible is ready, this brief is for the craft.

Read first: the world bible (compass-nova-world.html) and Nova's basics (compass-nova-character.html).

Deliverables

What we need from you.

1 · design

Nova character design

Final illustrated Nova: a clean turnaround (front, 3/4, side), plus an expression sheet (happy, thinking, excited/star-flare, sad/dim, pointing). Her signature: a compass body with a red needle and a gold star third-eye.

2 · design

The world

A world map illustration of the Onchain Sea, and key locations: the Fog, Newcomer's Harbor, and the islands (Earn Isle, Loan Cove, RWA Reef, Perps Peak, Rebalance Bay, Studio Lighthouse). Cozy, atmospheric backgrounds.

3 · cast

Supporting cast

The Travelers (a Wallet, a Fintech, a little robot Agent) and the Fog "villain". Simple, lovable, on-model with Nova.

4 · rig

A reusable Nova rig

Nova rigged so she can talk, blink, bob, point, and flare her star, driven by audio. Delivered in a tool the team can reuse per episode (see tech below).

Character spec

Who Nova is.

Form

a small star-compass, about beach-ball size, that floats. Round lavender body, soft face, big readable eyes, rosy cheeks, a wide friendly smile.

Signatures
(must keep)

a red needle on top (points, spins when thinking) and a gold star third-eye on her forehead (flares when something clicks).

Personality

warm, curious, a little cheeky, endlessly optimistic, cannot stand seeing anyone lost.

Motion tells

bobs while idle, needle spins when thinking, star flares + happy wobble when it clicks, dims a touch when someone is stuck.

Palette
lavender #5B47D6
soft face #EFEAFF
red needle #FF6B5E
gold star #FFD23F
ink features #16121F
Style & the quality bar

How it must feel.

Cozy, expressive 2D cartoon. Thick friendly outlines, squash-and-stretch, big emotion. Warm saturated world with depth, light and atmosphere (drifting fog, twinkling stars, gentle waves). It must feel hand-made and alive, never a flat corporate explainer with clip-art.

Pixar-short warmth Duolingo's Duo friendliness Headspace's cozy hand-made feel a Studio Ghibli sky
The six things that make it chafa, avoid all
stiff sliding movement· flat clip-art· generic stock· robotic voices· a character that looks different every video· no sound design
Technical · the rig

So we can reuse her.

The whole point is that after your work, our team produces episodes without you. Pick the pipeline that fits, we are open, but the rig must be audio-drivable and repeatable:

Option Best for What we get
Adobe Character Animator Talking-head Nova, fast turnaround Real-time lip-sync + expressions from voice, live puppet the team drives
Rive Crisp vector, web + app, tiny files Interactive rig: bob, blink, point, star-flare, embeddable on the site
After Effects (+ Duik/RubberHose) Premium 2D film look The richest animation; templated scenes we can refill per episode

Voice: we drive Nova with ElevenLabs. Deliver: source files, an on-model guide, and a short "how to make a new episode" doc so the team is self-sufficient. Output formats: 1:1 and 9:16, plus a web-embeddable Nova (Rive/Lottie) if possible.
