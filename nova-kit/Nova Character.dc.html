Compass Labs · Compass Learn · the education mascot

Nova.

A little star-compass, about beach-ball size, who floats above the world. Half compass, half star.

"Hi! I'm Nova. Lost in the fog? Don't worry, that's literally my whole job. Point the needle, and I'll get you there."

01 · Construction

How she is built.

One circle body, one face plate two-thirds its width, the needle mounted at true north, the star sitting on the forehead above the eyes. Keep these ratios and she stays on-model at any size.

Body

Round lavender, thick ink outline, one inner compass ring with three cream ticks at E, W and S.

Face

A soft plate dropped slightly below centre. Big readable eyes on the eye line, rosy cheeks, wide friendly smile.

Needle

Red half always leads. Pivots on an ink hub at the top of the body; it is her pointing hand, she has no arms.

Star

Gold five-point third-eye on the forehead, above the eyes, never off-centre. It is the only thing that glows.

02 · Expression sheet

Five faces, one Nova.

Everything she feels is carried by four parts: eyes, mouth, needle angle and star brightness. Nothing else moves.

Happy

Default. Bobs, blinks, needle swaying gently.

Curious

Brows up, eyes bright, needle spinning. Eager to figure it out, never worried.

It clicks

Star flare plus a happy wobble. The payoff beat.

Dim

Someone is stuck. She dims, she never scowls.

Pointing

The route beat. Needle out, eyes follow it.

04 · Motion tells

How she moves.

Bobs while idle

She never stands still and never slides. Weight floats, it does not translate.

Needle spins when thinking

A full clean rotation, then it lands on the answer.

Star flares when it clicks

Flare plus a happy wobble. This is the beat the viewer remembers.

Dims when someone is stuck

Sympathy, not sadness. She comes back up the moment the fog parts.
