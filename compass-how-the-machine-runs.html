Why it never stalls · two engines

A clock and triggers. That's the whole secret.

🕐

Engine 1 · The Clock

A weekly rhythm that runs no matter what. Monday decide, mid-week publish, Friday activate. The feed is never dead — even in a quiet week.

⚡

Engine 2 · The Triggers

When something happens (a ship, a win, an objection), whoever saw it flags it → Norte turns it into content. Real events become posts without extra work.

Content is never invented from zero. It's either the clock (routine) or a trigger (a real event). Both hand the hard part to Norte. The machine literally cannot go blank.

What starts it each week

Two moments turn the key.

FRIDAY · THE SWEEP

Gin (20 min): Norte pulls the week's signals from Slack + Attio into one list. This makes sure nothing's forgotten.

MONDAY · THE DECISION

Gin + Eli (30 min): pick the week's content, assign owners. Norte drafts from there. The one standing meeting.

So it can't stall: the clock runs no matter what, and Friday's sweep catches what people miss. There's no version where the machine goes blank waiting for someone's idea.
