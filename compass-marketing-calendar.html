The weekly rhythm (produces all four rows at once)

Same four moments, every week.

Mon · Triage (20m)

Pick the week's topics across all streams. Unblock sources.

Wed · Fact review (30m)

One validator per product, one batch, for everything shipping.

Thu · Production block (90m)

Record screens + human together. Queue Nova animations and HeyGen avatars with Norte.

Fri · Publish (20m)

Ship to hub, cut social clips, drop into campaigns and the API emails, capture one metric.

Why education starts Week 3: the source of truth, Nova and the scripts need to be locked first, and that is Weeks 1 and 2. Meanwhile Norte campaigns, social and the API get moving in Week 2, so momentum never stops. When education launches in Week 3 it is already polished, not rushed. Four rows, one calendar, one engine.
